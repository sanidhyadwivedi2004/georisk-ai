# GeoRisk AI - Data Sources Overview

> **Status:** Candidate/Planned Data Sources — to be verified during Phase 0.

This document lists the intended open and public data sources targeted for ingestion into GeoRisk AI.

---

## Intended Data Sources

### 1. GDELT (Global Data on Events, Location, and Tone)
* **Domain**: Geopolitical Event & News Discovery
* **Role**: Ingestion of real-world global news events, conflict monitoring, and tone analysis.

### 2. EIA (U.S. Energy Information Administration)
* **Domain**: Energy Statistics
* **Role**: Energy production, consumption, trade flows, and historical fuel pricing datasets.

### 3. OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries)
* **Domain**: Petroleum Statistics
* **Role**: Reference data on crude oil market trends, production quotas, and official market reports.

### 4. UN Comtrade (United Nations International Trade Statistics Database)
* **Domain**: International Trade Data
* **Role**: Bilateral trade statistics for energy commodities (HS codes for crude oil, LNG, refined products).

### 5. OFAC (Office of Foreign Assets Control - U.S. Department of the Treasury)
* **Domain**: Sanctions & Regulatory Data
* **Role**: Tracking sanctioned entities, vessels, and jurisdictions affecting energy shipping and trade.

### 6. World Bank Data
* **Domain**: Macroeconomic & Country Indicators
* **Role**: Country risk indicators, GDP, energy dependency ratios, and economic resilience metrics.

### 7. Global Energy Monitor (GEM)
* **Domain**: Energy Infrastructure Tracker
* **Role**: Asset-level spatial locations and attributes for pipelines, oil/gas terminals, refineries, and power stations.

### 8. OpenStreetMap (OSM)
* **Domain**: Geographic & Infrastructure Context
* **Role**: Detailed spatial context for ports, transportation networks, and maritime boundaries.

### 9. Natural Earth
* **Domain**: Geographic Boundaries & Basemaps
* **Role**: Cultural and physical vector datasets for country boundaries, disputed territories, and coastline geometries.
