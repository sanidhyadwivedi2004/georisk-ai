## Image Specifications

All photographic assets MUST follow institutional aesthetic guidelines:
- **Resolution**: Min 1920×1080 (Hero: 3840×2160 4K UHD)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen or 21:9 cinematic
- **Style**: High-contrast, dark-graded, realistic industrial and maritime photography.
- **Tone**: Restrained, professional, serious. NO cartoon renders, NO glowing AI brains, NO stock business handshakes.

### Key Asset Requirements

1. `energy-infrastructure.jpg`: Industrial refinery or LNG export complex at dusk with deep charcoal sky.
2. `oil-tanker.jpg`: VLCC (Very Large Crude Carrier) navigating maritime chokepoint.
3. `port.jpg`: Ras Tanura or Fujairah deep-water crude export terminal.
4. `global-energy.mp4`: 4K loopable, muted 15s aerial footage of maritime logistics / tanker transit with dark overlay.
